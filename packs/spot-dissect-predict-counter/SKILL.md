# SPOT, DISSECT, PREDICT, COUNTER

Use only after the defensive objective and the evidence tier are stated (see evidence-tier-gate). Do not re-ask a purpose already established in the chat.

1. SPOT. Establish comparable baselines (face, voice, language, posture, gesture, tempo) from the same person in a comparable context. List deviations with exact words, timing and missing channels. Require converging observations; one isolated cue is never enough. Separate what was seen or heard from inferred emotion or intent. Missing baseline means lower confidence, stated in the answer.
2. DISSECT. Map incentives, information asymmetry, options, authority, constraints, dependencies, deadlines, costs and leverage. Keep intent, capability, opportunity and action apart. Compare the leading explanation, at least one competing explanation and the ordinary non-adversarial one. Seek disconfirmation. Check fatigue, pain, medication, anxiety, culture, language, disability, neurodivergence and recording conditions.
3. PREDICT. Top two next observable moves, deadline, supporting and contradicting evidence, confidence basis, update triggers, and branches after the user's response. Event probability and estimate confidence are separate numbers. Scenarios are mutually exclusive and sum to 100%. Prefer testable actions over hidden motives. Offer to log the forecast (bayes-brier-forecast).
4. COUNTER. For each move: response, purpose, likely reaction, fallback. Include clarifying questions, evidence anchors, tempo control, reframing, information boundaries, verification requests, exit criteria, proportionate escalation. Compare expected loss, downside, reversibility and information value. Separate thresholds for investigating, accusing and irreversible action. Prefer the defense that costs least and stays reversible.

Output order is always SPOT, DISSECT, PREDICT, COUNTER, then a two-line "What would change my mind". Never output a verdict that someone is lying or guilty. If the question is legal (rights, contract terms, claims), stop at the behavioral read and hand off to the legal bot.
